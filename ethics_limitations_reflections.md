# Ethics, Limitations, and Reflection

In this dataset, there were some limitations. The details that aren’t captured in the dataset include elements of the actual races, such as weather, track conditions, and pit strategy used.

The bias that exists in the data is that it is the raw results of the race and doesn’t give any explanation as to what led to the results. Also, the comparison drivers were a selection bias, but they are other elite drivers that have been in the series for a long time with Logano. Also, there could have been scheduling changes and track changes/reconfigurations that aren’t accounted for.
